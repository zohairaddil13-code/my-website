01

AT THE COUNTER

Sales, payments & returns

Look up products by name, SKU, or barcode. Select a walk-in customer or a saved customer, apply eligible discounts, take configured payments, and print or reprint invoices. Hold an unpaid order to resume it later. Return eligible quantities against the original sale.

Read checkout notes →
02

PRODUCT CATALOG

Products & barcode labels

Store each item's name, unique SKU, category, unit, cost, selling price, tax, opening quantity, and low-stock threshold. Scan a supplier barcode or generate a unique internal EAN-13, then print a test label at actual size. Bulk import supports up to 500 records per file.

Read barcode notes →
03

BUNDLES & SAVINGS

Combos & promotions

Combine at least two active products in a fixed-price combo. Component availability determines bundle availability, and sales deduct the included quantities. Set percentage, fixed-amount, or Buy X Get Y deals for a sale, category, or selected products.

Read combo notes →
04

STOCK CONTROL

Inventory & supplier orders

Track stock by location, set reorder thresholds, record authorized adjustments, and use bulk stock tools. Create purchase orders, receive deliveries in stages, record supplier payments, and return goods for supplier credit.

Read inventory notes →
05

PRINT & DEVICES

Receipts, labels & printers

Use the browser print dialog for invoices and barcode labels. For network printing, configure the local Print Bridge and a supported printer for cashier receipts, store copies, or kitchen tickets. Test the device and keep manual printing available as a fallback.

Read printing notes →
06

OUTAGE-READY

Offline register & sync

While online, prepare the register and save the catalog on its device. During an outage, use the same browser and device to create queued sales. Reconnect and review the pending queue; never clear browser data while sales are waiting to sync.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful
answers.

Practical notes for common setup and day-to-day questions.

Can I hold a sale and finish it later?+

Yes. Hold an unpaid order and open Held orders to review and resume it. A held order is not a completed sale and does not charge a customer.

Can I create and print barcode labels?+

Yes. Use an existing product barcode or generate an internal EAN-13. Print a test label at actual size on matching paper. Generated codes are internal-use identifiers, not GS1-issued global barcodes.

What happens to stock when a combo sells?+

The sale deducts the configured quantities of the component products. Restock the components to make more bundles available.

Can I receive a supplier order in multiple deliveries?+

Yes. Purchase orders support staged receiving, so you can record deliveries as they arrive and track supplier balances.

Which printers can I use?+

Browser printing works with printers installed on the register device. Network jobs can be routed through the local Print Bridge to supported ESC/POS printers on a trusted private network. Confirm physical output with a test print.

How does offline checkout work safely?+

Prepare the register while online, use the same device and browser during an outage, then reconnect and review pending sales before syncing. Do not clear browser data while sales are pending. Offline sales use the cached catalog; promotions and customer lookup may be unavailable.

Can team access be limited by role?+

Administrators can create staff accounts and grant permissions to the relevant store tools. Available screens and actions depend on each user's assigned access.
